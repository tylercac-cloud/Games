## Fulfillment stack (how we actually deliver)

| Layer | Tool | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Automation engine | Make.com (start) or n8n (later, self-hosted, cheaper at scale) | Client pays nothing extra; runs under our account or theirs |
| Email | Client's existing Gmail / Outlook | Reminders come *from the firm*, which is why clients respond |
| Files | Client's existing Drive / SharePoint / Dropbox | Watch folders per client per month |
| Drafting brain | Claude API | Generates the personalized reminder text within their approved template |
| Tracking | Google Sheet or Airtable free tier | The "dashboard" clients see in v1 — the demo.html look comes later |

**Division of labor per client:** You do the kickoff email Q&A (their checklist, their tools, their voice — 20 min of typing). I design the workflow, write every scenario/branch, and draft the templates in our chat; you paste/click it into Make and connect their accounts (~2–3 hrs first time, ~1 hr once practiced). You send the "you're live" email. I write the check-in sequences.

**Realistic delivery time per client:** 3–5 hrs of your time in week one, dropping to ~2 as it templatizes. At $950, that's $190–300/hr effective. That's the business.
