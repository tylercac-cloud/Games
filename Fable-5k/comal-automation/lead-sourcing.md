# Lead Sourcing — 300 Texas Firms in ~3 Hours

Target profile: bookkeeping & accounting firms, **2–15 staff**, Texas. Owner/partner is the buyer.
Avoid: solo preparers (no budget), 25+ staff firms (committees, long cycles), Big-4-anything.

**No fabricated lists.** Every contact gets found, then verified. Bounces kill deliverability.

---

## Source 1 — Google Maps (best quality, most work)

Search per metro: `bookkeeping firm Austin`, `accounting firm San Antonio`, `CPA firm Fort Worth`, plus `bookkeeper {{suburb}}` for the ring cities. San Marcos → New Braunfels → Waco sized firms are ideal: real businesses, less pitched than Austin/Dallas cores.

For each: firm name, website, city. Skip franchises (H&R Block etc.).
Speed option: Outscraper or Apify Google Maps scrapers (~$10–30 for hundreds of rows including websites/phones). Manual works too — ~60/hr once you're in rhythm.

## Source 2 — Texas State Board of Public Accountancy

Public firm-license lookup at tsbpa.texas.gov → search registered firms by city. Confirms legitimacy and gives official firm names. Cross-reference, don't email blind from it.

## Source 3 — TXCPA chapter directories

Texas Society of CPAs has ~20 local chapters (Austin, San Antonio, Fort Worth, etc.), many with public member/firm listings. Members are engaged practitioners — good targets.

## Source 4 — Apollo.io (fastest, medium quality)

Free tier → filters: Industry = Accounting; Location = Texas; Employees = 1–20; Title = Owner, Partner, Principal, President. Export what the free credits allow, verify everything.

---

## Finding the email

Priority order:
1. Website contact page → a named person's email (best)
2. Pattern guess: `first@domain.com`, `first.last@domain.com` → confirm with a verifier
3. Apollo/Hunter.io lookup
4. `info@` only as last resort (worse reply rates)

## Verification (non-negotiable)

Run the full list through NeverBounce or ZeroBounce (~$8–15 per 1,000). **Delete every "invalid" and "catch-all risky."** A 5%+ bounce rate can get the sending domain blacklisted in week one.

## The spreadsheet

Columns: `first_name | last_name | firm_name | city | email | website | source | personalization_note | status`

The `personalization_note` is one specific thing per firm ("does CAS for restaurants", "hiring a bookkeeper on Indeed" — a hiring post = capacity pain = best signal there is). I can help draft these in batches: paste me 20 websites' worth of notes and I'll turn them into opening lines.

## Weekly rhythm

- Week 1: build 300, verify, load into the sending tool while warmup runs
- Ongoing: add 75–100/week (one 45-min session — or hand me the raw scrape and I'll clean/format it)
- Track reply rate per metro and per source; we double down where replies come from
