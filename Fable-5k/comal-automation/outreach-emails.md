# Cold Email Sequence — Texas Accounting & Bookkeeping Firms

**Rules that make this work:**
- Under 90 words per email. Plain text. No images, no attachments, one link max (the demo video, emails 2+ only).
- Line 1 is personalized per firm: `{{first_name}}`, `{{firm_name}}`, `{{city}}` minimum. Better: one specific detail ("saw you're taking on CAS clients").
- Send 20–30/day per inbox, Tue–Thu best. Warm the inbox 2–3 weeks first (every sending tool has built-in warmup — turn it on day one).
- SPF, DKIM, DMARC configured or nothing else matters.
- CAN-SPAM: your real physical address in the footer, honor every opt-out immediately, no deceptive subject lines. (Not legal advice — read the FTC's CAN-SPAM guide once.)

# Reply Playbook (closing without calls)

**"Interested / tell me more"**
> Great — the short version: we connect to the email and folder system you already use (Gmail/Outlook, Drive/SharePoint). We map your document checklist per client, and the system requests, reminds, tracks, and escalates automatically. Setup is $950 flat, live in about a week, and month one of support is included. Two questions and I can scope yours exactly: (1) roughly how many monthly bookkeeping clients? (2) what do you use for email and file storage?

**"How much?"**
> $950 one-time for the Document Chaser (most firms start here), $1,450 for the Front Office package (adds client onboarding + weekly status digests). Optional care plan $295/mo after the first month. No contracts, no per-seat pricing.

**"Is our client data safe?"**
> Fair question — it's the right one to ask. The automation runs inside your existing accounts (your email, your file storage); we don't warehouse client documents on our side. You get admin access to everything we build, and we'll sign your standard confidentiality agreement before touching anything.

**"We already use [Karbon/Canopy/TaxDome/etc.]"**
> Those are good practice-management tools — this isn't a replacement. Most firms on them still chase documents manually because clients ignore portal emails. We layer persistent, personalized follow-up on top of what you have. If your current setup already gets documents in on time without anyone nudging, you genuinely don't need me.

**"Not right now / busy season"**
> Completely understand. Reply "later" and I'll check in after the deadline crunch — and if you want it live *before* next busy season, the week to set it up is now. Either way, no more emails from me until then.

**Opt-out / "no"**
> Remove immediately, reply once: "Done — you won't hear from me again. Good luck this season."
