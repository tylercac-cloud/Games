# Demo Video Script — ~4 minutes

Setup: open `demo.html` full-screen in a clean browser window (no bookmarks bar). Record with Loom, camera bubble ON (small) — a face makes cold email trust jump. Talk like you talk. Two takes max; imperfect and human beats polished.

The demo shows a **fictional firm** ("Hill Country Bookkeeping") — say so on camera. Honesty reads as confidence.

---

**[0:00 — on the dashboard, don't touch anything yet]**

> "Hi, I'm {{name}}. I used to be the COO of a company here in Texas, and I now build back-office automation for small accounting and bookkeeping firms. This is a four-minute look at the first thing I set up for every firm: automated document collection. What you're seeing is a demo firm with made-up data — but the workflow is exactly what you'd get."

**[0:25 — sweep cursor across the stat strip]**

> "It's the 25th, mid-close. This firm has fourteen clients in the monthly close — nine are fully in, five are still missing documents. Twenty-three items outstanding. Normally, somebody on your team is chasing every one of those by hand."

**[0:45 — point at the client table, hover a 'Waiting' row]**

> "Every client, every missing item, how long it's been sitting. Green means done, amber means the system is on it, red means it's been escalated to a human — that's the only time anyone at the firm thinks about this."

**[1:10 — click 'Lone Star Landscaping']**

> "Here's Lone Star Landscaping — missing their July bank statement and fuel receipts, six days now. Look at the timeline: day zero, the request went out automatically. Day three, a friendly nudge. Today, a firmer reminder — and if nothing lands by day ten, it escalates to you with the full history."

**[1:45 — point at the AI-drafted reminder panel as it types]**

> "And this is the part clients actually respond to. Every reminder is drafted fresh — it names the exact documents, the exact period, in *your firm's* tone. You approve the templates once; after that it runs. This isn't a portal notification your clients have learned to ignore. It's a normal email, from your address, that reads like a person wrote it."

**[2:20 — click 'Regenerate' once]**

> "Different client, different situation, different email — automatically."

**[2:35 — click the 'What this replaces' toggle]**

> "What this replaces: about 45 minutes a day of someone's copy-paste follow-up, the 'wait, did we ever get their payroll report?' moments, and the awkward crunch when a client shows up on the 28th with a shoebox."

**[3:00 — back to full dashboard]**

> "Setup is $950 flat. It runs on the email and file storage you already use — Gmail or Outlook, Drive or SharePoint — so there's nothing new for your clients to log into. You're live about a week after you say go, and the first month of support is included."

**[3:25 — face the camera]**

> "If this looks useful, just reply to my email. Everything — questions, scoping, setup — happens over email, on your schedule. No sales call, no meeting. I'm taking on two founding firms at half price in exchange for a testimonial. Thanks for watching."

**[cut — total ~3:45]**
