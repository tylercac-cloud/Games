Your first session
  1. Use START-WINDOWS.bat (Python 3 required) and keep its window open. Use the same browser and address each time. The window prints Live data: OK for both Coinbase checks; a FAIL line names the cause and the fix.
  2. Optional: connect a View-only Coinbase key (see private/README.txt) so Capital plan fills equity, cash, open risk and fees itself.
  3. Crypta, your helper, is the round button at the bottom right of both tools. For her own taskbar window open 127.0.0.1:8765/crypta and choose Install in Edge or Chrome.
  4. Both tools show a Live market strip (BTC/ETH/SOL last price, 24h change and range, 24h USD volume, bid/ask spread), refreshed every 30 seconds. It is context only; nothing in the study uses it.
  5. Open the daily desk and try Synthetic preview. Then use Scan daily markets after 00:05 UTC. Failed or incomplete data must remain labeled that way.
  6. Open Capital plan. Enter current equity, cash, high-water mark, open risk and your actual fee assumptions. Values are manual and can become stale.
  7. Download each full scan. Export the daily desk backup after observations. If you use the strategy lab, export its separate register backup too.
Backups and continuity

The two tools retain their own records to prevent incompatible saves from overwriting one another. Import your earlier Claude register into the strategy lab; import the v7 checkpoint backup into the daily desk. Record each real trade in one journal only. The ZIP contains the software and audit history, not your browser’s personal records. Keep the downloaded JSON files alongside it. Opening the HTML directly uses different storage from the local launcher.

What the locked study means

H1 uses SMA 20/120. H2 uses a 60-day breakout and 30-day exit channel. Both remain unchanged, correlated, paper-only hypotheses. Their recorded forward interval is September 22, 2026 to September 22, 2027. Missed observations must be recorded as late reconstructions, never backdated as if they were observed live. A local fingerprint verifies consistency, not independent timestamping.
