Execution audit · one fixed rule

Load data and choose a strategy, parameters, exits and mechanism in Test first. This uses those settings with next-open fills. Each audit is logged as another configuration; changing cost assumptions is another look at the data.

Accounting diagnostic · no edge verdictEntries and signal exits use the next open. Stops may fill worse after a gap. If both stop and target are touched, stop wins. Open positions are liquidated at the final close. No leverage, cash interest, market impact or exchange lot-size model.

Use fees from your venue/account and order preview. Immediate fills generally use taker fees; maker execution is not guaranteed. Slippage inputs include spread and adverse fill movement. Allocation stays fixed as a share of available cash; the risk planner is separate.

Your search budget

Set this before testing anything. Given how much data you have, there is a hard limit on how many configurations you can try before a fake Sharpe of your target size becomes the expected outcome. Past that point you are not researching, you are sampling noise.

Minimum Backtest Length, Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado & Zhu (2014). Their worked figure — five years of data supports about 45 independent configurations at a target Sharpe of 1 — reproduces exactly here. Because parameter variants of one rule are highly correlated, raw trial count overstates independence; the correlation field converts M raw trials into effective independent trials.

Position sizing under uncertainty

Kelly assumes you know your win rate exactly. You don't — you have an estimate with a confidence interval. This sizes off the lower bound of that interval, not the point estimate.

Trial register

Every configuration you test, permanently. This count is what deflates your Sharpe — the register is the correction.

Manual save/restore (works even when downloads don't). This box always holds your current state as plain text. Before closing the tab: tap it, Select All, Copy, and paste into Notes or a message to yourself. To restore: paste your saved text in here, then tap "Import from text".
Import merges rather than replaces — entries already present are skipped. That direction is deliberate: losing register entries understates how many configurations you've searched, which inflates every Deflated Sharpe afterwards.
